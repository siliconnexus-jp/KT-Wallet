`base` is redrawn rather than copied. Base's brand kit
(`base/brand-kit`, `logo/TheSquare/Digital/Base_square_blue.svg`) defines the
symbol as a rounded square filling the frame, `fill: blue` (#0000FF), corner
radius 101.12/1280 of the side — so it reproduces exactly from those numbers,
sized to sit inside the circular clip the icon widget applies. The mark really
is a plain blue square; that is not a missing asset.

All artwork is normalised to 128×128 PNG with alpha, matching the original
cryptocurrency-icons set. The marks remain the trademarks of their respective
projects and are used here only to identify the chain or token they name.
